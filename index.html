About ExactaRobotics

Advanced Calibration &
Accuracy Control

At ExactaRobotics, our services focus on identifying, modeling, and compensating for geometric, non-geometric, and thermal errors that affect robot performance in manufacturing and automation environments.

Using state-of-the-art measurement systems (such as laser trackers, photogrammetry, and 3D vision sensors) and model-based optimization techniques, we provide customized calibration tailored to each robot type and task.

Reduce Errors

Minimize positional and orientation errors in complex robotic systems.

Improve Precision

Enhance tool-path precision for machining and assembly.

Coordination

Enable multi-robot coordination with high repeatability.
